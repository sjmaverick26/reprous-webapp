Our Story

Why ReproUs exists

We started ReproUs because too many people get their first "real" reproductive health info from a rumor, not a resource. Two people, one shared frustration, and a plan to make sure the next generation doesn't have to guess.

Our Mission

Free, honest reproductive health education — for anyone who's never had access to it.

No paywalls, no shame, no gatekeeping. Just clear information and a real community, built by people who remember exactly what it felt like to not know where else to ask.

Always free

Every workshop, article, and lesson — no cost, ever.

Judgment-free

Every question is a fair question here.

Built on community

Real stories and real people, not just a database of facts.

Spark

Myths & Facts

Tap a card to flip it

Myth

You can't get pregnant during your period.

Fact

It's less likely, but sperm can survive several days — it's still possible.

Myth

Irregular periods always mean something's wrong.

Fact

Many things affect regularity — stress, diet, sleep. Persistent irregularity is worth checking, but it's not automatically a problem.

Myth

Only girls need to learn about periods.

Fact

Everyone benefits from understanding it — friends, partners, siblings included.

Myth

PCOS means you can't get pregnant.

Fact

PCOS can make it harder, but many people with PCOS do get pregnant, sometimes with support.

Myth

You'll know right away if you have an STI.

Fact

Many STIs have no visible symptoms — regular testing is the only way to know for sure.

Myth

Cramps just mean you have low pain tolerance.

Fact

Severe cramps can signal something like endometriosis — persistent, intense pain is worth mentioning to a doctor.
